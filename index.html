What I build

A B-rep knows it's a cylinder. A mesh only knows it's triangles.

That difference decides everything downstream. Recognise a feature, estimate a cost, flag a manufacturability problem — each one depends on topology the mesh has already discarded. I work at the level where it still exists.

CAD-native machine learning

Networks that consume faces, edges and coedges directly — surface type, convexity, uv-grid samples, and the topology graph that binds them. No mesh in the middle.

B-rep · UV-Net · Graph networks

Pipelines that survive real files

STEP files in the wild are non-manifold, unclosed, and full of faces with no evaluable geometry. I build extraction pipelines that detect those cases and handle them, rather than failing on them overnight.

STEP · OpenCascade · Data engineering

Manufacturability and cost

Feature recognition, setup planning, and quoting models that connect geometry to what a part actually costs to cut — and flag the features that make it unmakeable before anyone commits to a price.

CNC · DFM · Cost modelling
